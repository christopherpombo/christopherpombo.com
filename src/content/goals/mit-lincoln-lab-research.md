Over the academic year, my team did undergraduate research with MIT Lincoln Laboratory on making air refueling scheduling algorithms more resilient in contested environments.

We placed 2nd of 9 teams at the 14th Annual Sustainment Innovation Competition (sponsored by Lockheed Martin) in Suffolk, VA, competing against the Naval Academy and West Point, and helped USAFA win the Best Team Award. We also presented the research at the Naval Academy's Capstone Day.

Thanks to MIT Lincoln Laboratory, Ben Kallemyn, and Lt Col Taylor Leonard for their mentorship.
