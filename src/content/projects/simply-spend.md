## The problem

Most money apps start by asking for a bank login, then spread the answer to a simple question — where did my money go this month? — across tabs and dashboards. I built Simply Spend to answer that question in one glance, without connecting anything.

## Who it's for

People who'd rather log spending by hand than link their accounts, want to see the month at a glance, and want their financial data to stay on their own phone.

## Key features

- **Three-mode calendar.** Flip the calendar between Spent, Earned, and Net to see every day — or every month of the year — from the angle you need.
- **Swipeable chart cards.** This week's running total against your usual pace, and a by-category breakdown, one swipe apart. An "Exclude fixed" switch takes rent and other fixed costs out of the picture.
- **Recurring transactions.** Set up rent, paychecks, and subscriptions once and they repeat on their own.
- **Notes.** Attach a note to any transaction, so "$18.50" still means something next month.
- **CSV export.** Export every transaction as a CSV from Settings and send it wherever you like.
- **Budgets** are coming in a later release.

## Private by design

Everything stays on the device. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics or tracking. The full details are in the [privacy policy](/simply-spend/privacy).

## Built with

Swift and SwiftUI, with SwiftData for on-device storage.
